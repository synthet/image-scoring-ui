# Synth app icon family

The Synth family icons moved to **[synthet/synth-brand](https://github.com/synthet/synth-brand)**, at commit `be5f947` and later, with their history:

- the family rules and mark registry: [`docs/ICON_FAMILY.md`](https://github.com/synthet/synth-brand/blob/main/docs/ICON_FAMILY.md)
- the masters: [`icons/projects/`](https://github.com/synthet/synth-brand/tree/main/icons/projects)
- the renderer, tokens and templates: [`icons/`](https://github.com/synthet/synth-brand/tree/main/icons)

This repo keeps its own rendered icon in `assets/`. The icon system lives in its own repo so the fleet-wide marks don't depend on this design-token package.
